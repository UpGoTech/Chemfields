    Chemicel®

    Chemicel® is the first product that was launched by Chemfield and has since been widely recognized as a value based and consistent MCC product. It has been the preferred choice of MCC for tablet manufacturing of top multinationals around the globe. Chemfield manufactures a range of grades under its brand name Chemicel® to meet specific customer requirements.
