    About Chemfield

    Being the pioneers of spray dried Microcrystalline Cellulose (MCC) in India over 20 years ago, Chemfield has since established itself as a research based, quality driven MCC manufacturer. Our expertise, dedication and drive have helped us to continuously push boundaries to be recognized as a global MCC player. Situated in Nagpur, Chemfield is centrally located which helps minimize logistics and enables quick service time to customers.

    Infrastructure

    Chemfield’s new manufacturing facility of MCC was designed and constructed by the world-renowned NNE Pharmaplan and complies to Indian FDA (Schedule M), WHO, US FDA and EU-GMP guidelines, as well as Chemfield's internal quality standards. The plant has a fully closed and automated process from start to finish, eliminating manhandling of the product and avoiding the risk of any product contamination. Furthermore, the plant is equipped with modern technology and the best equipment to ensure the production of streamlined, consistent and high quality MCC. The facility was commissioned in 2013 and has been successfully audited by top multinational pharmaceutical companies worldwide.
