    Overview

    Chemfield manufactures and supplies high-quality and consistent MCC by adhering to the following aspects:

    • Maintaining consistency and strict input acceptance criteria in quality of all raw materials
    • Maintaining operation parameters within the defined three sigma range
    • Continuous and rigorous training sessions to all employees which include on the job training, procedural training, and cGMP training
    • Stringent testing of frequencies and in-process limits

    Statement on Business Continuity

    Chemfield recognizes the importance of the safety of business and supplies and hence maintains more than one production line as a policy of business continuity.
