    About the Group

    Chemfield Cellulose is part of a first generation, family-run business house called Field Group (FG). From its humble beginnings 30 years ago, FG has established itself in multiple high growth sectors in distribution, mining and manufacturing. FG topline currently exceeds USD 50 million and the company is poised on a major growth trajectory with special focus on Excipient Development & Manufacturing.

    Message from Managing Director

    Being India’s first spray dried MCC manufacturer, our goal is to continue to be pioneers in the industry while staying true to our values of safety, quality and consistency. With this in mind, we have been the first MCC manufacturer globally to have successfully been audited by EXCiPACT. We are now undertaking another mission “Straight Line 2020” where Chemfield will achieve extreme lot-to-lot consistency in all its products by narrowing internal specs by 10% YOY. In addition to this, we have now added services for development, documentary, regulatory and stability filing for end formulations as required by customers. Thus Chemfield strives to always be a consistent and reliable solution provider to our clients.

    - Anil Taneja
