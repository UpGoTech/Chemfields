    Overview

    Chemfield through partnership has established a state-of-the-art facility in the city of Mumbai for formulation R&D and new product development. Our team of scientists come from an extensive pharmaceutical research background and are available for all application trouble-shooting, excipient performance and functionality measurements.

    R&D has helped Chemfield to achieve particle size engineering to ensure superior functionality of Primecel amongst all available MCC. These studies are available on special request. A similar approach is being adopted to come up with co-processed excipients to further support our clients. Thus, key focus areas for 2019 will be in enhanced functional MCC and co-processed excipients.
